# Mario's Mask Beta 0.4.0

## New

- **Choose a game mode when you build your ROM.** The patcher now offers two
  ways to play:
  - **Standard** (default): no Enhanced Mode. Firing Mario from the Bow costs
    5 arrows.
  - **Classic**: Enhanced Mode on C-Up, and firing Mario from the Bow costs
    1 arrow, as in earlier releases.
- **Challenge mode is coming soon.** It appears in the patcher but can't be
  selected yet.

## Get the game

Use the [web patcher](https://msmfai.github.io/marios-mask/) with your own ROMs. All processing happens locally on your device. Download `MariosMaskBuilder-web.html` below for the same patcher offline.

Bring your own NTSC Nintendo 64 ROMs for Super Mario 64, Majora's Mask, and Ocarina of Time. Any NTSC revision of Ocarina of Time may be used.
