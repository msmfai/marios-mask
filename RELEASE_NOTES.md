# Mario's Mask Experimental 0.2.1-experimental.20261024.g99c44f3

This is an **experimental** release: it is published without testing or QA, may be broken in ways a stable release is not, and will be removed once newer experimental releases replace it. For the tested release, use the latest stable release, [Beta 0.2.0](https://github.com/msmfai/marios-mask/releases/latest).

## What's new since Beta 0.2.0

- **Final Hours in Mario music mode.** With the Mario soundtrack selected, the last six hours before the moon falls are now scored with the Endless Stairs theme.

## Get this build

The [web patcher](https://msmfai.github.io/marios-mask/) always builds the latest **stable** release, not this one. To build this experimental release, download `MariosMaskBuilder-web.html` below and open it in your browser, or use one of the standalone builders. All processing happens locally on your device.

Bring your own NTSC Nintendo 64 ROMs for Super Mario 64, Majora's Mask, and Ocarina of Time. Any NTSC revision of Ocarina of Time may be used.
