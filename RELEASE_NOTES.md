# Mario's Mask Beta 0.2.3

## Fixes

- **Winning the Gorman Brothers' race no longer softlocks.** After beating them
  as Mario, the conversation froze on "You saw that." It now finishes, and the
  brothers hand over Garo's Mask (or milk, if you already have the mask) as in
  the original game, on every day of the cycle.
  ([#70](https://github.com/msmfai/marios-mask/issues/70))

## Get the game

Use the [web patcher](https://msmfai.github.io/marios-mask/) with your own ROMs. All processing happens locally on your device. Download `MariosMaskBuilder-web.html` below for the same patcher offline.

Bring your own NTSC Nintendo 64 ROMs for Super Mario 64, Majora's Mask, and Ocarina of Time. Any NTSC revision of Ocarina of Time may be used.
