Bring your own USA copies of Super Mario 64 and Majora's Mask. The builder combines them locally.

A different hero in the same world

Termina, with Mario's movement

Mario's Mask brings Mario's movement, attacks, voice, animations, and playful physics from Super Mario 64 into the world and adventure of Majora's Mask. Termina's story, three-day cycle, quests, dungeons, and characters remain at the heart of the game.

Find the Brother's Mask to become Mario, explore familiar places with a new move set, and remove it whenever you want to return to Link.
